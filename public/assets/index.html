How it works
          
        

        

          

            01
            
Paste a link

            
Drop a Spotify or Apple Music profile URL — or type an artist name — and pick the right match. No login, no API keys.

          

          

            02
            
Preview every field

            
See exactly what was scraped: genre, bio, top tracks with previews, albums, similar artists, concerts, stats — with ✓/✗ availability.

          

          

            03
            
Auto-fill & go

            
Confirm and the onboarding questions (Q1–Q12) and EPK are pre-filled. Import alone satisfies North Star criteria 2–4.

          

        

      


      
      

        
What gets auto-filled
          
        

        
Every field is typed, editable and confirmed by the artist — nothing is imported silently. Each provider contributes what it has; the union wins.

        

          

            

            
Genre

            
primaryGenreName from Apple Music / iTunes fills Q1 — the one field Spotify no longer exposes.

            Q1 · Apple
          

          

            

            
Influencing artists

            
Related artists (Spotify) + similar artists (Apple) become Q3 picks, capped at five editable chips.

            Q3 · Both
          

          

            

            
Bio → EPK Story

            
Curated biography becomes the Q8 bio-builder draft (placeholder promo bios filtered out), editable before save.

            Q8 · Both
          

          

            

            
Profile pic

            
Spotify avatar or 1500px Apple image becomes the EPK profile pic — rehosted, confirmed, and counted toward North Star #2.

            EPK · Both
          

          

            

            
Music & albums

            
Top tracks with 30-second previews plus albums/singles pre-fill the EPK Music section — a full release grid, one click.

            EPK · Both
          

          

            

            
Concerts & stats

            
Upcoming concerts (city + date) suggest Q10 performances; followers, monthly listeners and world rank add EPK credibility.

            Q10 · Spotify
          

        

      


      
      

        
Provider capabilities
          
        

        
Two engines, one union: import takes each field from whichever provider has it.

        

          

            
Spotify

            

              
✓Biography, avatar image

              
✓Top tracks + 30s previews

              
✓Albums & singles

              
✓Related artists

              
✓Upcoming concerts (city/date)

              
✓Followers, listeners, world rank

              
✓External links

              
✗Genre (removed from public data)

              
✗Gender / AI / lyrical flags

            

            
Engine: spotifyscraper — anonymous token, no API key, read-only public data.

          

          

            
Apple Music

            

              
✓Genre — primaryGenreName (Q1)

              
✓Curated biography (placeholder-filtered)

              
✓1500px artist image

              
✓Top songs + previews

              
✓Albums with artwork + release year

              
✓Similar artists

              
✓Region hint (storefront match)

              
✗Concerts / follower stats

              
✗Gender / AI / lyrical flags

            

            
Engine: iTunes Search/Lookup (free) + music.apple.com page scrape — ported from the Navidrome plugin.

          

        

      


      
      

        
Opportunity mapping
          
        

        
Opportunities carry attributes (gender, AI/non-AI, lyrical/instrumental). Each maps to an artist answer — unknown signals stay in the "Either" tier, never hidden.

        

          

            NEW · Q0e
            
Gender-specific

            
Optional gender answer (private, matching-only) ranks female/male-targeted calls; undeclared artists see them in the generic tier.

          

          

            NEW · upload flag
            
AI vs Non-AI songs

            
A per-track "made with AI" flag routes tracks to AI-only or human-only opportunities; undeclared = both, no ranking penalty.

          

          

            Q0b role
            
Lyrical vs Instrumental

            
Role derives the default (Lyricist/Rapper → lyrical, Instrumentalist/DJ → instrumental); a per-track flag overrides it.
